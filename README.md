# 🎣 FISHAO Reborn Wiki 🐟

**Welcome to the FISHAO Reborn Wiki!** 🌴

A fan-made community wiki dedicated to the original **FISHAO** fishing game.

Explore the world of FISHAO, rediscover familiar locations, and find helpful information about fish, fishing equipment, quests, and more!

## 🌊 Explore the World of FISHAO

- 🐟 **Fish** — Discover different fish species and learn where to find them.
- 🗺️ **Locations** — Explore fishing areas, including Pinheira Beach.
- 🎣 **Fishing Equipment** — Learn about rods, bait, and other equipment.
- 📜 **Quests** — Find information about challenges and adventures.
- 🏆 **Achievements** — Explore goals and collectibles.
- 💎 **Tips & Guides** — Helpful information for new and returning players.

## 🌴 About This Project

FISHAO Reborn Wiki is an independent, fan-made project created to preserve and share information about the original FISHAO experience.

Our goal is to build a welcoming place where the FISHAO community can rediscover the game and its memories.
